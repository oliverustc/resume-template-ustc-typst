Bundled Source Han fonts
=======================

Source Han Sans SC, release 2.005R:
  Official upstream: https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-sans
  Complete copyright notice and SIL Open Font License 1.1:
    fonts/source-han-sans/OFL.txt

Source Han Serif SC, release 2.003R:
  Official upstream: https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-serif
  Complete copyright notice and SIL Open Font License 1.1:
    fonts/source-han-serif/OFL.txt

Regular and Bold OpenType font files are included unchanged from the
official releases. File sources, release versions, sizes, and SHA-256
hashes are recorded in fonts/manifest.json.
